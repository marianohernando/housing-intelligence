# Sales & Commercial Validation Pipeline

## ICP
Promotoras/inmobiliarias medianas first.

## Funnel
- target account;
- discovery;
- data/problem validation;
- paid/unpaid pilot;
- report;
- PRO trial;
- subscription.

## Evidence to collect
- current workflow;
- cost of existing analysis;
- decision frequency;
- willingness to pay;
- procurement constraints;
- required exports/integrations.

## Gate before pricing final
At least several real conversations per ICP; do not finalize from intuition alone.
